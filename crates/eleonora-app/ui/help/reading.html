The sidebar

From the top:

InboxEverything in the Inbox of every one of your own accounts, together (shared mailboxes and the Online Archive have their own folders under Accounts and Shared Mailboxes).
Other Recent MailWhat the spam filter and your rules sorted out of the Inbox lately (marketing, notifications, mailing lists), never spam, so nothing new goes unseen. By default the last 48 hours, and 72 hours on Mondays to take in the weekend. Change the hours in Settings ▸ General ▸ Other Recent Mail.
All Mail - FilteredEvery account's real mail: everything except spam, marketing, notifications, mailing lists, trash, drafts, and what you sent. This is where Eleonora opens the first time.
All MailEvery message in the library, once each. Mailbox ▸ Hide Mail I Sent in All Mail (on by default) leaves out what you sent.
ViewsMessages gathered by what they are, wherever they're filed: Red Alert, Receipts, Invoices, Travel, and more. See Views.
AccountsEach account with its folder tree. Click the arrow to open or close it.
Shared MailboxesMicrosoft 365 mailboxes shared with you. See Shared mailboxes.
Calendar, Contacts, To DoSee Calendar, Contacts, and To Do.

Counts

Each folder shows how many messages it holds, and in bold how many are unread, for example 4,593 (6). When the sidebar is narrow, big numbers are shortened (2.3M, 4.5K) and then left out, so the names stay readable.

Right-click a folder or account

  • Mark All as Read: everything in it and in the folders under it. Over 500 messages, Eleonora asks first. The servers are told too.
  • New Mailbox…, New Mailbox Inside…, Rename…, Delete…: see Making, renaming, and deleting mailboxes.
  • Make it active / inactive, Remove from Eleonora… (shared mailboxes).

Opening a folder fetches its newest 50 messages from the server first, so it's up to date when you look.

The message list

Unread messages are bold. Marks beside each row show the status (replied, forwarded), priority, attachments (a paperclip, only for files really attached, not pictures shown in the message), a ⚑ for a message flagged as a To Do, and the message's labels in their colors.

Columns

Right-click the column headings (or Mailbox ▸ Columns…) to choose them: Who, From, To, Cc, Date, Added to Eleonora, Size, Number of attachments, Subject, Preview, Folders, Account, Mailing list, Message-ID, Status, Priority, and any header of the message (Add a header column…). Drag a heading to move it and its right edge to size it. Each look remembers its own columns; Reset brings back the usual ones.

Sorting

Click a column heading to sort by it (▲ / ▼ on the heading); click again for the other way. Dates, sizes, and counts sort newest or biggest first; words sort A to Z. Folders, Account, and header columns don't sort. The first time a very big list is sorted by a column other than Date (All Mail by Who, millions of messages) it can take a minute or more, and the status bar says it's sorting. After that the order is kept: the list opens at once in that order, even after you've looked at other lists, and when new mail arrives Eleonora sorts it again in the background and moves the rows into place when it's done.

Choosing several messages

ClickOne message.
Ctrl+clickAdd or take away one.
Shift+click, Shift+↑ / ↓A run of messages.
Ctrl+AEvery message in the list, even those not loaded yet.
The check boxesIn search results a column of check boxes comes first: tick a message's box to add it, untick it to take it away (the message shown stays the same), and Shift+click a box to tick the run from the last one. The box in the column's heading chooses every message found, or none when all were chosen; it's half ticked when some are. For other lists, right-click the column headings and add Check boxes.

Delete, Transfer, Copy To, Filter Messages, and Forward act on all of them. Forwarding several sends one message with each attached whole. Over 100 messages, Eleonora asks first.

New mail

New mail slips in at the top without moving what you're reading: the message you have selected stays in its place on the screen.

Keys

↑ / ↓ move through the list, Delete deletes, and Insert flags as a To Do.

Reading a message

Select a message to read it in the reading pane. It's marked read after a moment.

Above the message:

  • Fewer headers: subject, From, and Date on one line, without the Message, Text, Headers, and Source tabs, so more of the message shows. The choice holds for every message until you change it. Show all the headers to see the tabs again (F7 and View Source show them too).
  • Maximize: the message fills Eleonora's window as an inner window. Its buttons restore, minimize, pop out, or close it (Esc).
  • Pop out: the message in a window of its own.

Beside the sender: the copy button copies their email address, and the envelope opens a new message to them (not a reply). When the sender is one of your contacts, Edit contact opens Contacts at their contact, ready to change; when they aren't, Add contact opens a new contact with their name and address filled in, to finish and save.

The tabs show the Message, its plain Text, its Headers, and its Source (the raw message). Message ▸ Show All Headers (F7; "Blah Blah Blah" in Classic mode) shows every header.

When Eleonora holds more than one original copy of a message (for example your old Eudora file and Microsoft 365's copy), its Originals are listed; click one to see its own bytes in Source.

Safe by design

  • Messages are shown in a sealed frame: scripts in mail never run.
  • Pictures from the web are blocked until you choose; see Pictures from the web.
  • A message the checks find to be a fraud shows a red warning, and its links and pictures are switched off. See Fraud and phishing.

Links

Clicking a web link opens it in your browser. Point at a link first to see where it really goes. A mailto: link starts a new message.

Invitations

A message holding a meeting invitation shows a bar with Accept, Tentative, Decline, and Propose New Time. See Invitations.

Linked payments

A payment and its receipt for the same amount within 30 minutes are linked; the message shows "Linked: Receipt…" or "Linked: Payment…", and a click opens the other.

Attachments

Attachments are listed under the message's headers, each with its file type's icon.

  • Click one to open it with your computer's program for that kind of file.
  • The arrow saves it where you choose.
  • Save All… saves every attachment into a folder.
  • Drag one out of Eleonora onto your desktop or a folder.

Files Eleonora opens go into the library's Attachments folder, in a folder for each year. A file that arrives again in another message isn't stored twice.

Every file Eleonora writes is marked as downloaded from the Internet, so Windows and the Mac treat it with the usual care. Eleonora never opens programs or scripts from a message, and never opens a fraud's attachments.

Pictures shown inside the message (logos, photos placed in the text) aren't listed as attachments on the Message tab; the Text, Headers, and Source tabs list everything.

Pictures from the web

Many messages load pictures from the web. Loading them can tell the sender when you opened the message, so Eleonora doesn't load them until you say so. A bar above the message says how many aren't shown:

  • Show pictures: this time only.
  • Always show from <address>: pictures from this sender load by themselves from now on.

Eleonora fetches the pictures itself and puts them into the message; the message never goes online. In Settings ▸ Mail ▸ Pictures from the web you can choose to keep fetched pictures on this computer (they then show offline, and after the sender deletes them), and remove senders from the "always show" list.

Senders whose pictures always show

Settings ▸ Mail ▸ Pictures from the web has a button, Senders whose pictures always show…, that opens the list in a window of its own:

  • Find narrows the list as you type. Each sender has Remove, and their messages ask first again from then on.
  • Add puts an address on the list, or @example.com for everyone at that domain.
  • Import… adds the addresses in a text or CSV file (any number a line, separated by commas, semicolons, or spaces; "Name <address>" is read as its address; lines starting with # are skipped). Addresses already on the list aren't added twice.
  • Export… saves the list as Eleonora picture senders.txt in the folder you choose, one address a line, ready to import on another computer or into another program.

Also show pictures from people in my Address Book, a checkbox on the same page, lets pictures load by themselves from anyone in your Address Book, without adding each one to the list.

The list and the checkbox travel to your other computers with your settings. A message Eleonora takes for a fraud never loads its pictures, whoever sent it.

Views

Views gather messages by what they are, wherever they're filed. A message can be in several Views. They aren't folders: nothing is moved.

Red AlertThings you must never miss: shut-off, default, and missed-payment notices, collections, lawsuits, tax and regulator notices (from senders that prove who they are), urgent mail from people you write to, and security advisories and patch notices for products you run.
Maybe SpamMail the spam filter wasn't sure about. It stays in your Inbox and is listed here; mark it Spam or Not Spam to teach the filter.
Payments ReceivedCard processors and payment services telling you money came in.
Payments SentYou paying: PayPal, banks, cards.
ReceiptsReceipts and order confirmations.
InvoicesInvoices and requests to be paid.
RenewalsSubscriptions and services renewing.
Bank TransactionsNotices from banks and payment services.
New AccountsWelcome and sign-up confirmations.
Verification CodesOne-time sign-in codes.
LegalLaw firms, court matters, and privileged and confidential mail.
GovernmentMail from government senders that prove who they are.
TravelReservations, itineraries, boarding passes, rides, and rentals.

Under each View, its messages are grouped by sender (Amazon, PayPal, Authorize.Net…), biggest first.

Each account has its own Views under it. The Views at the top of the sidebar combine the accounts you tick (right-click Views); shared mailboxes are left out at first.

Mail you sent and mail in a Trash folder aren't in any View. A message moved back out of the Trash returns to its Views.

Why is a message in a View?

Right-click it and choose Why in these Views? (also Special ▸ Why in these Views?). Each View works by points: a message needs 2 points, and the window lists what gave each one.

The Online Archive

A Microsoft 365 mailbox may have an Online Archive holding older mail. With Include the Online Archive on (Settings ▸ Downloading and sync), Eleonora brings all of it down: mail, and also the archive's calendar items, contacts, tasks, and notes, each as a readable entry in its folder.

The archive's folders join your mailbox's folders: the archive's Inbox shows as part of your Inbox, and a folder that exists only in the archive (such as Inbox_2021) shows by its own name. You can manage the whole mailbox as one without knowing what is archived.

Archive mail you move or sort in Eleonora stays in the archive on the server, in folders of the same layout inside it (for example Online Archive ▸ Bulk ▸ Marketing).

Each download pass says whether every item on the server is in Eleonora, or how many aren't yet.

Eleonora also keeps Microsoft's own copy of each archive item, exactly as Microsoft sent it, unless you turn that off: see Keeping original messages exactly.

Shared mailboxes

In Microsoft 365, mailboxes shared with you (support@, sales@, or a colleague's mailbox you were given) are chosen in Settings ▸ Accounts ▸ Shared mailboxes. The list there shows every mailbox in your organization you may open, so you can keep just the few you use in Eleonora even when you can open dozens.

  • Tick a mailbox to show it in Eleonora. It appears under Shared Mailboxes in the sidebar, inactive.
  • Untick one to take it out of Eleonora. It leaves the sidebar at once, and Eleonora removes the copies of its mail on this computer in the background (Activity shows how far it is). Mail that's also in another of your mailboxes stays there. Nothing changes on Microsoft's server: tick it again any time to bring it back. Right-click a shared mailbox in the sidebar for Remove from Eleonora… too.
  • Active mailboxes download in the background with your own mail. Inactive ones aren't checked: opening one of their folders and choosing Check Mail brings that folder up to date. Right-click a shared mailbox in the sidebar to make it active or inactive.
  • Find a mailbox narrows the list as you type.
  • Check Now asks Microsoft again which mailboxes you can open (about a minute: Microsoft offers no list, so Eleonora tries each mailbox in the directory).

Eleonora also looks again by itself once a day. A mailbox you've newly been given is never added by itself: Eleonora asks, once, and marks it New in the list. A mailbox you can no longer open stays in Eleonora with the mail already here, marked No access (in the sidebar too), and Eleonora stops checking it; untick it to remove it.

You can send from a shared mailbox's address: choose it in the message's From. Eleonora sends as the mailbox when you have that right, else on behalf of it.
